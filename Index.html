MAS7 Language Specifications

An artificial constructed language (conlang) engineered to alter cognitive parsing methods by eliminating rigid word ordering and relying on morphological suffixes and evidential markers.

Overview

In traditional human languages, comprehension relies heavily on the sequential order of words (e.g., Subject-Verb-Object). MAS7 completely isolates syntax from positioning. Instead, word relationships and source veracity are encoded directly onto each stem through stacked affixes, forcing speakers to retain an entire utterance in memory before assembling its meaning.

Cognitive Processing Rule

In MAS7, sentence elements carry complete grammatical autonomy. Order does not convey meaning—suffix markers dictate structural roles.

Architecture Mechanics

1. Mandatory Case Stacking

Nouns must take a case suffix to define their role in the sentence before any other modifier is attached.

Case Role Marker Function Example
Agentive -ok The entity performing the action Kaelok (The scout does...)
Patientive -em The entity receiving the action Kaelokem (The scout is acted upon)
Locative -ith The space, destination, or container Kaelokith (At/inside the scout)
Instrumental -us The tool or method used Kaelokus (Using the scout)

2. Evidentiality System

A speaker cannot make a statement in MAS7 without grammatically declaring their source of information. Evidential suffixes attach directly to verbs or root concepts.

-vax  :: Direct Visual Verification (Speaker physically witnessed the event)
-rin  :: Second-hand Transmogrification (Hearsay or reported statement)
-kor  :: Deductive Inference (Logical deduction based on circumstantial proof)
-mya  :: Intuitive / Speculative (Unverified hunch or dream)

3. Free-Floating Syntax Engine

Because every element carries its own structural role tag, sentence elements can appear in any sequence without altering meaning.

For example, the statement "The scout observed the target" can be written in six equivalent ways:

1. Kaelok Vraem Drakvax
2. Drakvax Vraem Kaelok
3. Vraem Kaelok Drakvax
4. Drakvax Kaelok Vraem
5. Kaelok Drakvax Vraem
6. Vraem Drakvax Kaelok
